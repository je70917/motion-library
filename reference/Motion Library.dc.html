Motion library

A working set of micro-interactions for the design team — soft, fluid timing applied to the everyday states a UI needs: buttons, forms, loaders, overlays and notifications. Each panel loops on its own — click one for the code.

03 — Loading & progress

Loading & progress

Spinner

A rotating crosshair rather than a ring — reads as instrumentation, not a stock loader.

Progress fill

Eased fill rather than linear — it starts eager and settles in, matching how real transfers feel.

Skeleton shimmer

Placeholder blocks for content that hasn't arrived yet — implies "still loading," not "empty."
